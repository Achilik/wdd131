Website Planning Document

Site Name

Giovani Catering School

I chose this name because it clearly identifies the website as a professional catering school focused on teaching culinary and catering skills. The name conveys trust, expertise, and hospitality — which aligns with the mission of the organization. It also works well for branding.

Optional Domain Name: giovani-cateringschool.com (example only)

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide information about Giovani Catering School, including its courses, admissions, facilities, and training programs. The website will serve as a central hub where potential students can learn about available catering and culinary programs, view class schedules, explore tuition information, and submit enrollment inquiries. It will also showcase what makes the school unique and highlight career opportunities in the catering field.

Scenarios

  • “How can I enroll in a catering program at Giovani Catering School?”
  • “What types of catering courses and certifications does the school offer?”
  • “What skills will I learn, and how long do the training programs last?”

Color Scheme

Selected Colors:

  • #3a7d44 (Deep Green) – Used for headings, navigation, and highlights.
  • #d4a017 (Gold) – Used for accents, buttons, and important icons.
  • #ffffff (White) – Main background and clean section areas.
  • #1b1b1b (Black) – Primary body text.

These colors reflect freshness, professionalism, and the culinary theme. Green symbolizes food, freshness, and growth, while gold reflects quality and excellence.

Typography

Selected Fonts:

  • Heading Font: Poppins – Used for titles, section headers, and hero text.
  • Body Font: Roboto – Used for paragraphs, descriptions, and general reading material.

These fonts were chosen because they are modern, clean, and highly readable for both mobile and desktop users.
